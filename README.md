## About

I'm a recent Honours BA Psychology graduate from York University (Cum Laude, Dean's Honour Roll) moving into HR and Talent Acquisition, with a specific pull toward the data side of the function: people analytics, HR technology, and evidence-based hiring decisions rather than gut calls.

Four years of research methods training (regression, mediation analysis, experimental design) turned out to translate directly into HR analytics. Psychometrics connects to assessment design. Social and cognitive psychology inform bias-aware interviewing. I'm comfortable in R, Jamovi, and JASP from that training, and I've spent recent months getting hands-on with the tools an HR or People Analytics team actually runs on: Power BI, Tableau, Salesforce, and SAP ERP.

**Open to:** HR Coordinator, Talent Acquisition Coordinator, People Operations Coordinator, and HR Analyst roles across the Greater Toronto Area.
